# Scope and style examples

## Preserve the main thread

Request: “从圆的切线讲到圆锥曲线的极线，并解释与偏导和 Hessian 的联系。”

Appropriate: derive the circle tangent and contact chord; generalize using homogenization and polarization; prove reciprocity and contact-chord properties; then use a short gradient/Taylor section to explain the analytical connection.

Inappropriate: turn the chapter into limits → derivatives → multivariable differentiability → implicit-function theorem, introducing conics only near the end. Even correct prerequisite material can displace the requested subject.

## Convert conversation into a formal statement

Conversational: “你说的办法没错，把平方拆成乘积，外点就是切点弦。”

Textbook: “设 P 为圆 x²+y²=r² 外一点。从 P 向圆作两条切线，切点为 A、B，则直线 AB 的方程为 x₀x+y₀y=r²。” Follow with the orthogonality proof.

Do not overcorrect into dense abstraction: establish the circle result before presenting a homogeneous symmetric matrix.

## Explain a connection with its limit

For a quadratic f and its polar expression T_P, derive
2T_P(X)=2f(P)+∇f(P)·(X−P).

At f(P)=0 and a regular point, T_P=0 agrees with the tangent equation. Away from the curve, distinguish the polar from the zero set of the Taylor linearization f(P)+∇f(P)·(X−P). Do not call the polar an ordinary first-order approximation.

For a conic center, the affine polar equation can have no finite solutions; explain the line at infinity when a unified polarity statement is needed. Use “coincides with the tangent”, not “degenerates into the tangent.”

## Transfer to another topic

Request: “用教材语言写均值不等式讲义，说明与凸函数的联系。”

Start with the inequalities and equality conditions, establish representative proofs and examples, and use convexity as a subsequent unifying interpretation. Do not replace the inequalities chapter with a full course on convex analysis. Definitions and theorem boxes must support this topic's mathematical dependencies, not copy the geometry chapter's section titles.
